AccentOne token, warm neutrals unchanged
The palette is two layers. The warm neutrals — paper, ink, hairline, muted — are fixed and carry the identity. Accent is a single value used in exactly five places: group label, list link, FAB, active nav item, and the primary button. Swap it and the app still looks like SetListArray.

How it resolves
1. User-picked accent, if set.

Derived per accent
