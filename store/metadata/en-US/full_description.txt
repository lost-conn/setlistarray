SetListArray is an offline-first book of the songs you know how to play.

Put a song in it, attach the chart you actually read from, and arrange the songs into setlists. That is the whole app. There is no account to make, nothing to sign in to, and nothing about your book leaves the phone.

WHAT A SONG HOLDS

A title and an artist are all that is asked for. Key, capo, tuning, tempo, length, tags, notes and how solid you are on it are all optional, and the form keeps them folded away until you want them.

CHARTS THAT OPEN WITH NO SIGNAL

Three kinds of chart attach to a song:

• PDFs, drawn a page at a time, with zoom and rotate. A photocopied page out of a fake book works as well as a clean export does.
• Webpages, captured for offline use. Paste in a link to a chord page and the app fetches it once, keeps what is worth keeping, and never asks the network about it again. You choose reader text or the whole page before a byte is saved.
• Lyrics and chords you type yourself, in a monospaced field, so the chords stay over the syllables you put them over.

A song can hold several charts. One of them is the primary one, and that is what the song opens on.

SETLISTS

Build a set out of your book, reorder it, and watch the running time add up beside it as you go. A song with no chart attached is flagged on the set while you are still at home, rather than when you are standing in front of people.

PERFORMANCE MODE

One tap starts the set. The chart fills the screen at a size you can read from a music stand, the top bar says where you are in the set, the bottom bar says what is next, and the screen can be held awake for as long as the set lasts.

SEARCH

One field searches titles, artists and setlists — and the text inside your charts, typed and captured alike. Half a line of the second verse is enough to find the song it belongs to.

THE LIBRARY IS YOURS TO CARRY

Export the whole book — database, charts and all — as a single zip file, and keep it wherever you keep things that matter. Import it back onto the same phone, or onto a new one.

NO ACCOUNT, NO SYNC, NO TRACKING

SetListArray asks Android for one permission, INTERNET, and uses it for exactly one thing: fetching a webpage you pasted in yourself, so that it still opens when the venue has no signal. There is no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no sign-in. Your songs live in the app's own private storage on the device, and they are backed up when you export them and not before.

IT CAN LOOK LIKE THE REST OF YOUR PHONE

The accent colour can follow your Material You wallpaper palette or be picked by hand, and dark mode can follow the system or be set outright. Performance mode has a darker theme of its own for a dim stage.
